Privacy Policy

App: NotToDo ／ Developer: kmachida12345 ／ Effective: October 18, 2026

This policy describes how the Android app "NotToDo" (the "App") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect or transmit any personal information to the developer or to any server. It does not obtain your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The titles, descriptions, creation dates, completion status, and completion dates of the items you enter are stored only in a database on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or any third party. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may download fonts (Roboto and Kiwi Maru) from Google Fonts through Google Play services' downloadable fonts feature. This request is handled by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy. It does not include any content you enter in the App.

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

4. Sharing of Information

Because the developer does not collect your information, none is shared with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us via GitHub Issues.
